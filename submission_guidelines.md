## 🚨 Minimum Submission Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

Submissions missing any of these will be at a serious disadvantage.

---

### 🔧 Core Requirements

- Use **OpenEnv (latest release)**  
  Build on top of the framework — do not reinvent the wheel.

- Provide a **working training script** using:
  - Unsloth, or  
  - Hugging Face TRL, or  
  - Any other RL framework  
  *(Preferably as a Colab notebook so judges can re-run it easily)*

- Show **evidence of actual training**, including:
  - Loss plots  
  - Reward curves  
  *(from a real training run)*

---

### 🎥 Documentation & Explanation

- Provide **one of the following**:
  - A mini-blog on Hugging Face, OR  
  - A < 2 minute YouTube video  

- This should explain:
  - What your environment does  
  - What you trained  

- Ensure **all materials are linked in your README.md** for easy access.

---

### 🚀 Deployment Requirement

- You **MUST push your environment to a Hugging Face Space**  
  - It should be discoverable  
  - It should be runnable  

---

### 📄 README Requirements

Your `README.md` must include:

- Problem motivation  
- Explanation of how the environment works  
- Results and observations  

- **Mandatory links:**
  - Link to your Hugging Face Space (environment)
  - Links to all additional materials:
    - Videos  
    - Blog posts  
    - Slides  
    - Presentations  

---

### ⚠️ Repository Guidelines

- **DO NOT upload large video files** to your HF repository  
- Instead:
  - Use **public URLs** for videos or external content
